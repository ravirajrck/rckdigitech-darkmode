Raviraj began his journey in 2015 while pursuing his college studies. Alongside academics, he started freelancing as a web developer and digital marketer. Working directly with local businesses, he sharpened his technical stack, launched digital channels, and built a solid foundation in online community building.

#CollegeDays #Freelancing
2019-22
Branch 02 • Growth & Expansion

Establishing a Training & Tech Hub

In 2019, Raviraj established his own training institute to mentor students in programming and modern technologies. While guiding aspiring developers to kickstart their careers, he simultaneously managed high-end freelance client projects, scaling both his technical authority and business acumen.

#Mentorship #TechHub
2022-25
Branch 03 • Enterprise Mastery

Corporate Software Engineering

Joining a premier IT firm in Indore as a Software Developer, Raviraj spent 3 years building large-scale applications for national and international clients. Leveraging expertise in Angular, robust UI/UX architecture, and enterprise digital strategies, he delivered mission-critical products before stepping into full-scale entrepreneurship.

#AngularExpert #EnterpriseIT
